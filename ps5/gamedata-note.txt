Liberty Recompiled needs your own copy of GTA IV (Xbox 360, USA, version 1.00)
and its v8 title update. None of the game comes with this title.

  gamedata/       the files extract-xiso extracted from your disc (default.xex,
                  common.rpf, xbox360.rpf, audio.rpf and the rest), or the disc
                  image (.iso) itself
  title-update/   the v8 title update: the TU file from your Xbox 360's hard drive
                  (Content/0000000000000000/545407F2/000B0000/), or its default.xexp.
                  Leave only that one file here (and this README).
  episodes/       optional: TLAD/ and TBOGT/, the episodes' files

On its first launch the title unpacks the game into game/ (several minutes, and
about twice the game's size in free space). After that it starts the game
directly, and gamedata/ and title-update/ can be emptied.

A game folder already installed by Liberty Recompiled on a PC (its
LibertyRecomp/game folder) can go in gamedata/ instead: it is used as it is.
